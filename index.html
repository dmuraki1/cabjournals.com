What cast and blast means

A cast and blast is a day that starts with a rod and ends with a shotgun. Fish early while the water's quiet, then swap the rod for a gun when the birds start moving. Around here the seasons line up to make it work.

I'm not a guide and I'm not selling anything on this site. It's a journal. If you fish or hunt the same water, you'll probably recognize some of it.
