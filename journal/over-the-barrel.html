Hunting

Over the barrel

Short clips from the camera mounted on the shotgun.

Every clip here is a few seconds long, and that's just how it goes. The bird comes in, you get one look, and it's over.

The camera sits on the barrel, so the dot is where the gun's pointed. It's a good way to see what you're doing wrong.

Written by Doug Muraki, owner of Sunfish Pool Service in Sacramento. More at dougmuraki.com.
