Fishing

The orca

An orca came up right next to the boat.

We were fishing halibut when an orca came up right next to the boat. It rolled once, close enough to see the white on its side, and kept going.

I got the phone out just in time. It's about ten seconds, and I'm glad I have it.

Written by Doug Muraki, owner of Sunfish Pool Service in Sacramento. More at dougmuraki.com.
