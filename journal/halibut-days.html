Halibut days

Clips from a halibut trip, mostly the fight and the landing.

Halibut don't come up easy. Most of these clips are somebody leaning on a bent rod for a long time, and then a big flat fish coming over the rail.

The last one is from the back of the boat, when something showed up off the stern and worked the surface for a while.

Written by Doug Muraki, owner of Sunfish Pool Service in Sacramento. More at dougmuraki.com.
