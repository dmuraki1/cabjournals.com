A salmon trip

A few days on the water with the boys and a dock full of fish.

This one was a few days on a charter boat with the boys, rain gear on, pulling salmon and the occasional lingcod over the rail.

The dock shot at the end of each day is the one everybody takes. The kid with the king was the picture of the trip.
