Fishing

The biggest bass

The best largemouth we have put in the boat, pulled from years of photos.

I went back through every fishing picture I have and pulled out the biggest bass. Some are mine, a lot of them are the boys', and a couple belong to friends who out-fished me that day.

No weights listed because half of them never touched a scale. The pictures tell you enough.

Written by Doug Muraki, owner of Sunfish Pool Service in Sacramento. More at dougmuraki.com.
