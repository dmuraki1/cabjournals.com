Hunting

From the blind

Phone clips from the tules, the field and the wait in between.

Most of a duck hunt is sitting still and watching the sky, and that's what these are. Sunrise over the water, decoys, and a lot of nothing until it isn't.

My son's in a few of them. The one with the drake is him, and the two in the dry field are him too.

Written by Doug Muraki, owner of Sunfish Pool Service in Sacramento. More at dougmuraki.com.
