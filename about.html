I'm Doug Muraki. I own Sunfish Pool Service in Sacramento and I run it full time. Before this I spent twenty years in service work, and I'd wanted to build something of my own for a long time.

Cast and Blast Journals is a personal site. I keep track of fishing and hunting trips here, with photos and the occasional video, so I have one place to look back on them. Nothing here is for sale.

If you're looking for the business, it's at sunfishswim.com. If you want the rest of me in one place, that's dougmuraki.com.
